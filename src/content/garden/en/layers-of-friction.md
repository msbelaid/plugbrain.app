Most apps want to feel smooth. Every tap is one less step, every screen opens faster, every scroll goes on forever.

That's not what we want in PlugBrain.

We add friction on purpose. Layer after layer, each one a chance to stop and think again.

The first layer is the math challenge itself. A few seconds where your brain has to wake up.

Then there are two buttons. Leaving the app is the main one, front and center. Solving the challenge is the second choice.

To answer, you have to tap the field and type. The keyboard used to open on its own. Now it waits for you to choose.

Even a correct answer doesn't send you straight back. PlugBrain asks one more time: unlock the app for a few minutes, or close it and stay focused?

And we don't make both choices look equal. "Stay focused" is the big, bright button, the one your thumb finds first. Unlocking is the quieter option. That's on purpose.

Most apps minimize friction. We want to maximize it.

Because the goal isn't to get you back into the app faster. It's to help you put your phone down.
