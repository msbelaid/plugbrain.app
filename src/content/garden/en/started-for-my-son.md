My son loved his tablet. It was the hardest thing in the house to put down.

He also struggled at school, especially with math. To him, numbers were a punishment, not a tool.

So I put the two problems together. What if the way back to his tablet went through a few simple sums?

I looked for an app that did this. I didn't find one that fit.

So I built my own. Pick the apps to lock. When one opens, a math problem appears. Solve it, and the app is yours again.

The first problems were easy, like 4 + 3 or 9 − 5. I wanted him to win.

Something shifted. Math stopped being homework and became a key. He was doing sums every day, without anyone asking.

I didn't need to ban the tablet. I needed to change what it asked of him.

That small app later became PlugBrain.
