Every October, developers around the world join Hacktoberfest. For a month, they look for open-source projects to help.

That year, I got PlugBrain ready. I wrote eighteen small issues, each one a clear first step for a newcomer.

Then the pull requests started.

Two new languages: Hindi and Russian.

Two new kinds of challenges: find the missing number, and squares.

A button to leave the challenge screen. Durations you can read at a glance. A clearer explanation of why the app needs its permissions. Fixes for bugs I had never seen.

By the end of the month, seven people had their code in PlugBrain. Most of them I had never met.

Reviewing their work taught me as much as writing my own.

PlugBrain was no longer my side project. It was ours.
