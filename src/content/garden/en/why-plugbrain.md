At first, the app was called MathLock.

It said what it did. But I wanted something catchy, a name that explains why the app exists.

So I made a list. Brainrot Killer. ConnectBrain. Plug Your Brain. Math Focus. And many more.

Brainrot is that foggy, drained feeling after hours of scrolling short, mindless content. Oxford even picked "brain rot" as its Word of the Year in 2024.

My choice went to PlugBrain.

It's short and easy to remember. And it says what the app asks of you: before you reach for your phone, plug your brain in.

I searched everywhere. No app had a similar name, and the domain was free.

On June 20, 2025, MathLock became PlugBrain.

Two days later, it was on its way to F-Droid.
