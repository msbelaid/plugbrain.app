PlugBrain worked. But it looked like what it was: an app designed by a developer.

Then [Hichem](https://github.com/HichemDahaoui), a designer from Algeria like me, offered to help.

He redrew the whole app in Figma. New colors. Calm teal instead of loud tones. Cards, buttons and number inputs, each one designed to be reused.

A design is only a picture until someone builds it.

So I cut it into small issues. One for the color system. One for the challenge card. One for the buttons.

Contributors picked them up, one by one, from different countries. Each piece came with screenshots, so we could check it against the design.

It took most of 2026.

In October, version 1.6 shipped with the new look: a step-by-step onboarding and a calm, simple dashboard.
